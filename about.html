About Us

The store next door, run like it should be.

SMR Grocery Store is a small, everyday grocery shop located inside SMR Vinay Iconia in Kondapur, Hyderabad — built for residents who'd rather call in an order than make a supermarket run.

Our story

SMR Grocery Store exists for one simple reason: not every grocery run needs a trip out of the society. Whether it's milk for the morning, vegetables for tonight's dinner, or a forgotten packet of salt, we keep the everyday essentials in stock and within a short walk — or a short wait — of every home in SMR Vinay Iconia.

We're not trying to be a supermarket. We're trying to be the shelf you wish was closer — fruits and vegetables, dairy, daily staples, snacks, and household basics, restocked regularly and ready to send over the moment you call.
